MiniDisc Manager for Mac 0.34.5 (beta) - READ ME FIRST
=========================================

Manage the music on Hi-MD and standard MiniDiscs from your Mac: add tracks, rip CDs, play discs, edit titles, groups and pictures,
record from streaming sites, and more.

WHAT YOU NEED
-------------
* A Mac with an Apple silicon chip (M1, M2, M3, M4 or newer). Intel Macs are not supported.
* macOS 13 (Ventura) or newer.
* A Hi-MD or MiniDisc recorder with a USB cable (for example the Sony MZ-NH700, MZ-RH1, MZ-NH900 ...).
* An internet connection is only needed for lookups (album names, covers, lyrics) and for recording from streaming sites.


INSTALLING - 4 STEPS
--------------------
1. DRAG THE APP
   Drag "MiniDisc Manager" onto the "Applications" folder in this window. Then eject this disk image
   (right-click its icon on the desktop, or in the Finder sidebar, and choose Eject).

2. OPEN IT THE FIRST TIME (one extra step, because the app is not signed by Apple)
   Open the Applications folder and double-click "MiniDisc Manager". macOS says it cannot check the app for malware
   (or that the developer cannot be verified). Click "Done" (not "Move to Bin"), then:
     * macOS 15 (Sequoia) or newer: open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message
       "MiniDisc Manager was blocked to protect your Mac", click "Open Anyway", and enter your password.
       Then open MiniDisc Manager again and click "Open" if it asks once more.
     * macOS 13 or 14: right-click (or hold Control and click) "MiniDisc Manager" and choose "Open", then click "Open" again.
   You only have to do this once.
   If macOS says the app is "damaged": it is not; this is how macOS treats downloaded, unsigned apps. Open Terminal and run
       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/MiniDisc Manager.app"
   and open the app again.

3. ONE-TIME SETUP OF THE BACKGROUND SERVICE
   The first time it starts, MiniDisc Manager explains that it needs to install a small background service, and asks
   you to press "Set up". macOS then asks for YOUR MAC PASSWORD (an administrator account is needed).
   Why: macOS does not let ordinary programs talk to a recorder over USB. The service does that on the app's behalf.
   It only talks to MiniDisc recorders and to the files you give the app. It takes a few seconds, and the app
   starts again by itself. The same happens after an update that changes the service.

4. PLUG IN THE RECORDER
   Connect the recorder with its USB cable and put a disc in it. Wait about 45 seconds the first time (macOS is still
   looking at the disc; the app waits for it on purpose and shows a countdown). The disc then appears in the app.
   * A standard (SP/LP) MiniDisc in a Hi-MD recorder shows as "(standard disc)", a Hi-MD disc as "(Hi-MD disc)".
   * The disc does not appear on the desktop: the service keeps macOS from mounting it, because macOS would write to it.


THINGS macOS WILL ASK FOR LATER
-------------------------------
* "Screen & System Audio Recording" - only when you record from Bandcamp, SoundCloud, Apple Music and similar windows
  inside MiniDisc Manager. The app tells you when it is needed and opens the right page of System Settings.
  Switch on "MiniDisc Manager", then quit the app (MiniDisc Manager > Quit) and open it again.
  Nothing is recorded from your screen: only the sound of the window is captured.
* Files and folders - macOS may ask whether the app may read your Music or Downloads folder when you add tracks.
* If you rip audio CDs: connect a USB CD drive. If the Music app opens when you insert a CD, close it, or turn this off in
  System Settings > CDs & DVDs (set "When you insert a music CD" to "Ignore"): Music keeps the drive busy.


UPDATING
--------
Replace the app in Applications with the new one. If the service changed, the app offers to update it and asks for your
password again.


REMOVING MINIDISC MANAGER
-------------------------
1. UNPLUG THE RECORDER first.
2. Double-click "Uninstall MiniDisc Manager.command" in this disk image. Terminal opens and asks for your password.
   It removes the background service, the app and (if you say yes) the app's settings and saved recordings.
(Without it the app can simply be dragged to the Bin, but the background service would stay installed.)
To remove only the service by hand, in Terminal:
   sudo sh "/Applications/MiniDisc Manager.app/Contents/Frameworks/mac_uninstall.sh"


IF SOMETHING DOES NOT WORK
--------------------------
* "The background service is not running" / the recorder is not found:
  quit the app, unplug the recorder, wait 10 seconds, plug it in again, wait about a minute, and open the app.
  If it still fails, open the app again and choose "Set up" when it offers the service setup, or run the setup by hand:
   sudo sh "/Applications/MiniDisc Manager.app/Contents/Frameworks/mac_install.sh"
* The recorder shows up but nothing loads / "Reading the recorder" takes very long: this is normal for the first
  45 seconds after plugging in. If it stays stuck, unplug and replug the recorder.
* Playing a standard (SP) disc: the first track takes about 20 to 30 seconds to start (the disc has to spin up); the next
  tracks start at once because the app reads them in advance.
* Logs for support: /var/log/minidisc-manager.log (the service) and
  ~/Library/Application Support/MiniDiscManager/app.log (the app).


PRIVACY
-------
MiniDisc Manager works offline. It contacts the internet only when you ask for something that needs it (album and cover
lookups, lyrics, streaming-site windows, Yoto, casting to a TV or speaker on your network).
It sends no personal information, and it keeps no account of its own.
