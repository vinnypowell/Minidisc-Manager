MiniDisc Manager for Mac - version 0.36.1 (BETA)
====================================================================

Windows: an Update now button in the new-version notice (installed copy). Mac: no change except the version number; the Mac keeps the Open download page link.

Since 0.36.0 - New: new-version notice. Once a day the program asks this project's GitHub release page for the newest version and shows a small box if yours is older (Open download page, Later, Skip this version). Nothing about you is sent and nothing is installed by itself. Switch it off in Settings; Help > Check for updates asks straight away.

Since 0.35.2 - Fix: the bass (LFE) meter in the Surround channels display now moves; it is judged against its own loud level instead of the full-range channels'.

Since 0.35.1 - Fix: the program now starts even if the numpy library cannot be loaded (only the Surround channels display, binaural playback and the TV picture styles are switched off then).

Since 0.35.0 - New: Disc J-Cards (Recording box, next to Disc Stickers). It makes the J-card for the slim MiniDisc case (11 mm tab, 5.5 mm spine, 68 mm front, plus an
optional 66 mm fold-out panel with the track list) and a disc sticker, to print on any printer. "Print..." opens the page in Preview (press Cmd+P and print at
Actual Size), "Make printable PDF..." saves it with crop and fold marks, and "Save pictures..." saves 300 dpi PNGs. On a Hi-MD disc "Save to disc" stores the page
as JCARD.PDF, and an "Open J-Card" button then appears in Disc Information. The Disc labelling tab is now called Disc Stickers (unchanged otherwise).
French, German, Dutch and Spanish texts are included.

Intel Macs: there is no Intel version of 0.36.1. The experimental Intel build stays at 0.34.5 and is no longer updated.

Everything else is unchanged from 0.35.0.

Notes for 0.34.5 follow.

MiniDisc Manager for Mac - version 0.34.0 (first Mac release, BETA)
====================================================================

This is the first version of MiniDisc Manager for the Mac. It is a beta: the main things work and have been tested, but it has so far been tried on ONE
recorder (Sony MZ-NH700), ONE Mac (Mac mini M1, macOS 26) and a handful of discs. Please do not use it on a disc you cannot replace, and copy
anything precious off first.

Requirements: a Mac with an Apple silicon chip, macOS 13 or newer, a Hi-MD or MiniDisc recorder with a USB cable. The install steps are in "READ ME FIRST".
The app is NOT signed by Apple (see the install steps for the one-time "Open Anyway").

WHAT WORKS (tested on the Mac)
------------------------------
Hi-MD discs
* Reading the disc, playing tracks (including lyrics, the visualiser and 5.1 spatial tracks), adding tracks from files in PCM, ATRAC3 and ATRAC3+.
* Titles, artists, disc name, groups, album pictures, lyrics and the track list file; moving, renaming, splitting, combining, deleting, fade & level.
* Formatting, Disc Packages (.mdpack export and import), and reading and writing 5.1 / spatial audio.
* Lyrics: searching, saving, and "Listen" (the app listens to a track and writes timed lyrics itself, on your Mac, using its GPU; the 190 MB speech model
  is downloaded once, only when you ask).
* Adding tracks by recording from Bandcamp and SoundCloud windows inside the app, and from internet radio song by song (see the limits below).
Standard (SP / LP) MiniDiscs in a Hi-MD recorder such as the MZ-NH700
* Reading the disc, erasing it, adding tracks in SP, LP2 and LP4, playing tracks on the Mac (the app reads the audio back with the same optional
  firmware patch as the Windows version: the patch lives only in the recorder's memory and is cleared when you unplug it).
* Convert SP disc to Hi-MD.
CDs
* Ripping an audio CD to the disc, with titles from the disc and (online) MusicBrainz, and an album cover. The disc name becomes "Album: Artist".
Casting
* Playing to a Google TV / Chromecast, including 5.1 sound and the picture.

EXPERIMENTAL ON THE MAC
-----------------------
* Burn Audio CD uses macOS's own burner. The app reads the tracks, prepares the disc and starts the
  burn correctly, but it has NOT yet been confirmed with a real burn: the one test drive (a bus-powered slim USB drive) lost its USB connection
  every time it started to write. A drive that macOS supports, with enough power (a powered USB hub or a two-plug cable), should work. Try it on
  a rewritable disc (CD-RW) first.
* Saving or adding as MP3, lyric videos with text, and https radio in the stretch and HLS modes: the Mac's audio tool now has an MP3 encoder, https and
  the text filters, but these have not been tried inside the app yet.

NOT AVAILABLE ON THE MAC YET (the buttons may still be visible)
--------------------------------------------------------------
* The Bluetooth speaker chooser and Bluetooth label printing (Polaroid Hi-Print, HP Sprocket).
* Cloning a standard (SP) disc to another disc.
* Sonos casting has not been tried on the Mac.

KNOWN QUIRKS
------------
* Starting a standard-disc track for the first time takes about 20 to 30 seconds (the disc has to spin up); following tracks start at once.
* After plugging the recorder in, the app waits about 45 seconds before it uses it. This is deliberate: macOS is still looking at the disc and
  taking the recorder over sooner can leave it stuck.
* The window may change size by itself for a few seconds right after the disc first appears.
* The volume of a cast to a TV cannot be turned very low.
* The timings shown while ripping a CD are approximate.
* A bus-powered CD drive that is short of power can drop off the USB port while reading or burning; plug it straight into the Mac (both plugs of a two-plug cable) or use a powered USB hub.
* Recording from a streaming-site window needs macOS's "Screen & System Audio Recording" permission for MiniDisc Manager (the app asks for it).
