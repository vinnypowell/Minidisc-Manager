Disc Management

Arrange
Tick tracks first: Move and Split take one (Split can cut it at many points at once), Combine takes two or more, Edit details takes any number. You can also drag a track by its number to a new place; ticked tracks move together. ⓘ How it works…
Fade & level
Tick one or more tracks, then press Fade & level: add a fade in and / or out, make them louder or quieter by a set amount, or even out their volume. The changed tracks are written next to the originals; nothing is deleted until you choose. Hi-MD PCM / ATRAC, or SP / LP on a standard disc (if the recorder can send the tracks back). ⓘ How it works…
Auto split & name
Tick one long track (SP / LP, or PCM / ATRAC on Hi-MD): the app finds the gaps between songs, splits it and names the new tracks. You check everything before it is kept. ⓘ How it works…
Groups
Group tracks that belong together, such as an album, so the recorder can play or skip them as one. Hi-MD discs for now. ⓘ How it works…
Clone
Copy a whole disc onto another disc of the same kind. ⓘ How it works…
Remove
Delete ticked tracks, or undo a rename or move. ⓘ How it works…
Format
Erase the whole disc and set it up empty again, as a standard disc or (on a Hi-MD recorder) as a Hi-MD disc. You type FORMAT to confirm. ⓘ How it works…

Disc Conversion

Convert SP Disc to Hi-MD
Turn a standard (SP / LP) disc into a Hi-MD disc: the tracks are saved on the PC first and written back in the format you choose. ⓘ How it works…
Convert LP2/4 Disc to SP
Players without LP support play LP2 / LP4 tracks as silence. Choose the tracks to rewrite as SP, in the same places on the disc. ⓘ How it works…
Nothing to convert on this disc. This card offers “Convert SP Disc to Hi-MD” for a standard disc in a Hi-MD recorder, and “Convert LP2/4 Disc to SP” when a standard disc has LP2 or LP4 tracks.

Disc Extras

Identify
Name tracks: search for the album online, paste a track list, or let the app listen to the audio (needs a free AcoustID key). ⓘ How it works…
Lyrics
Find lyrics online, keep them on the disc and read them in time with the music while a track plays. ⓘ How it works…
Video
Tick a track (or several, or all of them), find the best YouTube video and keep the link with the track: on this PC and, on a Hi-MD disc, in the track list stored on the disc. Tracks with a video show a small YT button. ⓘ How it works…
Check
A quick report: missing titles, artists and albums, duplicates, lyrics and free space. ⓘ How it works…
Tracks
Save the track list as a spreadsheet, or keep one on a Hi-MD disc and open it. ⓘ How it works…
Copy out
Copy tracks to your PC, burn an audio CD, or save them to a disc. ⓘ How it works…
